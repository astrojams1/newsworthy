## Authorized reply

Hello App Review team,

Thank you for reviewing Newsworthy. I would appreciate reconsideration under Guideline 4.2, with some additional context about its intended utility.

I have spoken with dozens of users who want to stay connected to world events but find conventional news apps overwhelming. Newsworthy’s simplicity is exactly what they are looking for: a brief check-in that lets them get on with their day. Adding more articles, feeds, or content to browse would undermine the benefit these users want.

Newsworthy provides a periodically updated, AI-generated assessment of news consequence: a score out of 10, one sentence explaining the current news, and an update time. The concise presentation is a deliberate product decision. Its ongoing utility comes from the changing assessment, rather than the volume of content available during a single visit.

The submitted app also includes native Home Screen widgets in small and medium sizes. These let users see the reading at a glance without opening the app or visiting a website. The physical-device recording supplied with our submission demonstrates both widget sizes alongside the app.

We understand that a simple interface must still provide adequate utility. We believe Newsworthy meets that standard by serving a specific, recurring need: staying connected to current events with minimal time and attention. The app intentionally has no scrolling feed, advertising, subscriptions, or engagement incentives.

Could you please reconsider the app with this use case and its native widget functionality in mind? If these do not sufficiently address the concern, we would appreciate clarification on what aspect of the utility is inadequate, so we can address it while preserving the experience our users value.

Thank you for your consideration.
