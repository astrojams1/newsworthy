# Google Play closed test

Play makes a personal developer account run a closed test with at least 12
testers opted in for 14 continuous days before it accepts an application for
production access. A tester who leaves before day 14 does not count, so recruit
15 to 20. Recruitment posts and replies: [the Reddit ledger](../docs/reddit/ledger.md).

## Set up

| Piece | State (2026-10-15) |
|---|---|
| Testers group | `newsworthy-testers@googlegroups.com`, <https://groups.google.com/g/newsworthy-testers>. Anyone can join; only managers see members, post or read conversations; listed on the web so the join page opens from a link |
| Closed track | Closed testing - Alpha. Testers: that Google Group. Feedback: the support email. Saved, not sent for review |
| Countries | Not chosen |
| Release | None. Needs the app content declarations, a price (the payments profile) and Play review |
| Opt-in link | `https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.astrojams.newsworthy` (Play's usual form); works only once the release is published |

Newsworthy is paid, and Google says closed testers must buy a paid app; internal
testers get it free but do not count. Play offers up to 500 paid-app promo codes
a quarter; whether they work before the app is published is unverified.

## Tester instructions

Send to each person who joins.

```
Thanks for testing Newsworthy.
1. Join with the Google account on your phone: https://groups.google.com/g/newsworthy-testers
2. Become a tester: https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.astrojams.newsworthy
3. Install from Play and keep it 14 days. Add the widget.
Problems: astrojams1@gmail.com
```
