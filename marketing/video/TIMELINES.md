# Creative rationale and exact timelines

All films are **1080 × 1920, 30 fps**. A and B are **22 seconds / 660 frames**; C is **24 seconds / 720 frames**. Thirty frames per second supports the intended measured editorial cadence, with brief accelerated movement in A. Every proposition and call to action is readable without audio.

The authoritative composition source is [`src/Films.tsx`](src/Films.tsx), with shared device/endcard treatment in [`src/design.tsx`](src/design.tsx). Times below use seconds; intervals include their start and exclude their end. Animation cues between frame times become visible on the next 30 fps frame. Labels are transcribed in their rendered uppercase; `<br>` indicates an intentional line break. Device text, recorded separately below, is part of the retained screenshot rather than campaign copy.

## A — The Anti-Feed

**Idea:** make “more” feel endless, then give the film a decisive stopping point. An accelerating column of the same word becomes an attention machine. The cut to “Enough.” provides the visual and sonic relief the product promises. Seven seconds with the authentic reading gives the viewer time to understand the object before the philosophy and price close the film.

| Time | Exact campaign copy | Picture and timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3.5 | Repeated `MORE.`; `The feed never says enough.` | Large type accelerates upward inside two fine vertical rules. Increasing motion softness after 2.2 s. The stationary hook remains readable. |
| 3.5–7 | `NEWSWORTHY / A DIFFERENT KIND OF NEWS APP` | Immediate cut to paper and empty space. |
| 3.85–7 | `Enough.` | Fades, sharpens and rises into place over 0.75 s. A dash grows from 4.2–5.4 s. |
| 7–11 | `World news. Rated.`; `RECORDED 1 OCT 2026` | Authentic light reading capture. Phone reveal settles over 1.15 s. |
| 11–14 | `One score. One sentence.`; `RECORDED 1 OCT 2026` | Same reading remains in place; only the editorial caption changes. |
| 14–18 | `STAY INFORMED. KEEP YOUR ATTENTION.`; `Stay informed.` | Cut to near-black, with a quiet stone dash. |
| 15.25–18 | `Not addicted.` | Second line reveals over 0.75 s. |
| 18–22 | Shared endcard; supporting copy `World news, rated<br>by significance.` | Paper endcard fades in over 0.65 s; held to the final frame. |

**Sound:** tactile repeating texture accelerates with the column. The 3.5 s cut initiates exactly half a second of digital silence, followed by sparse original tones. See the sound-design document for the measured master.

**Alternate opening:** `How much news is enough?` replaces `The feed never says enough.` during 0–3.5 s. All other images, words and timing remain unchanged.

## B — The $1 Proposition

**Idea:** put an almost absurdly small transaction against something valuable: attention. The oversized dollar figure and the tiny final period make the price feel definitive. A restrained receipt makes the purchase model tangible, then the dark native reading gives the viewer the product behind the proposition.

## C — Full Stop

**Idea:** an ending is an unusual feature to advertise. Start the film where most films finish, then explain the product's real boundary. The moving period travels through the graphic language, stops at the week limit, and becomes the brand dash. The work challenges the idea that a news product must always offer another item.

| Time | Exact campaign copy | Picture and timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4 | `A NEWS APP WITH AN ENDING.`; `The<br>end` with a separately animated period | A large typographic ending is the opening hook. The period begins to travel at 2.5 s. The words fade at 3.45–3.95 s; the dot expands at 3.65–4 s into the dark cut. |
| 4–7 | `NEWSWORTHY` | A dark field and a thin horizontal line. |
| 4.1–7 | `News doesn’t<br>have to be<br>endless.` | Text reveals over 0.75 s. A pale dot travels along the line from 4.35–6.3 s, then stops. |
| 7–12 | `First, a score and a sentence.`; `RECORDED 1 OCT 2026` | Native light reading capture; 1.15 s reveal. |
| 12–14 | `The past week, in one list.`; `RECORDED 1 OCT 2026` | Native timeline capture; 1.15 s reveal. This shows part of the authentic list, not its bottom. |
| 14–17 | `THE TIMELINE / ONE WEEK`; `7 days.`; `A list that ends.` | Editorial graphic outside the app: seven tick marks reveal, a line draws, and a terracotta dot reaches its endpoint by 16.2 s. The graphic explains the verified one-week limit. |
| 17–20 | `News.<br>Full stop` with a separately animated period; `WORLD NEWS, RATED BY SIGNIFICANCE.` | From 18.7–20 s the period travels toward the brand position and becomes a dash. It is not a second punctuation mark or a new app control. |

**Sound:** a low felt-like opening strike, long pauses, restrained punctuation tones, and a sparse final resolution. The final silence is allowed to remain quiet.

**Alternate opening:** `THE MOST RADICAL THING IN NEWS.` replaces `A NEWS APP WITH AN ENDING.` during 0–4 s. Other copy and timing remain unchanged.

## Recommendation and opening tests

**Lead with B.** After delivery, the owner reported: “B performed best by far.” That result supersedes the initial creative recommendation of A. Retain the original B as the control and test one opening-copy change at a time, preserving its price, purchase model, product sequence and CTA.

The initial recommendation favored A’s overload-to-relief contrast. B introduces the price immediately and holds the final offer for five seconds. Its stronger reported performance makes the direct offer the leading direction for further testing, but does not establish which element caused the result. No metric, figures, audience, spend or test conditions were supplied; no quantitative lift or purchase-specific outcome is claimed.

Each composition accepts `inputProps: {"hook":"alternate"}`. The render script exposes this as `--alternate`, for example:

```sh
node scripts/render.mjs --only=A --alternate
node scripts/render.mjs --only=B --alternate
node scripts/render.mjs --only=C --alternate
```

Use the same audience, placement and remaining film when comparing each alternate opening. No performance claim is attached to an untested hook.
