## Synthesis and decisions

1. **OPP-001 remains rank 1/validate, not approved for development.** Existing
   evidence supports verification; new E-01/06 challenge the idea that a link
   solves trust or depth, and E-07 shows why link accessibility belongs in the
   feasibility criterion. Keep problem confidence medium and solution low.
   First match supporting reporting to the displayed sentence, then assess
   whether the content is accessible without excessive friction. This run did
   not audit production history or create a citation pipeline.
2. **OPP-002 remains rank 2/candidate.** The app now has an introduction and
   linked help, but neither establishes comprehension. E-04/05 show contrasting
   actual preferences; E-10 records failed relevance controls in another app.
   Check whether people understand the existing product before adding prose or
   any About screen. Local/personalized/full-article feeds stay outside scope.
3. **OPP-003 remains rank 3, now validate the implemented presentation.** Preserve
   the distinction and test it with unchanged-sentence/new-check examples, across
   the two-second transition and widgets. No new verified temporal-confusion
   incident was found; do not infer absence of demand from weak search yield.
   Problem/solution confidence stays low for any further presentation change.
4. **OPP-004 remains preserve.** E-02/03/05/08/09 strengthen the case for a
   bounded, intelligible check and intentional opt-in. Optional alerts/timeline
   already exist; do not re-propose or remove them on this evidence. Preserve
   off defaults and matching controls; no engagement goals follow.

No new opportunity ID, removal or product feature is justified. No advertising,
subscription, feed, source-list policy, rating-prompt or scoring change is made.
The two Support edits correct obsolete statements; they add no capability.
Problem confidence is not adoption, prevalence or willingness-to-pay evidence.

## Limitations and retrospective

- New counts comprise seven HN observations in seven clusters and three store
  observations in two clusters. Apple/Google stores are one source family, not
  independent families for quota purposes. Revisited evidence is excluded from
  new counts. Cross-site identity and shared-incident independence are unknown.
- Reddit and vendor support were not verified. HN overselects technically minded
  readers. French reviews add language/region diversity but do not establish
  portability, nationality or frequency. No accessibility study was performed.
- Date-aware search did not itself prove recency. Eight new accounts have exact
  recent comment/update dates; one is historical and one undated. Original-body
  reads do not verify reported competitor defects. No deleted/flagged text was
  reconstructed, and no promotion was counted as independent demand.
- Previous experiment: seek two independent dated non-Kagi source/temporal
  discussions including a non-English family. **Partly met**: French dated
  source-access accounts and undated Inshorts control evidence; not two dated
  non-Kagi discussions about source verification/temporal wording. No new
  temporal-confusion account was verified. Retain that gap explicitly.
- Productive: supported public item/review interfaces recovered original text
  and exact timestamps when general search was unhelpful. Deliberate contrary
  reading exposed citation insufficiency, depth preferences and positive brevity.
  Unproductive: repeated generic Bing results, blocked engines and broad HN
  lexical matches. Stopped retries after recording gates; did not use proxies or
  browser-verification workarounds.
- Narrow playbook update: original public API bodies can verify a lead, but
  search excerpts cannot; Apple RSS `updated` is not initial publication; empty
  store feeds are access gaps. These lessons follow the E-07/08 locators and
  the revisited HN date, not invented source diversity. Skill rules unchanged.
- Next search experiment: seek two dated non-Kagi consumer accounts specifically
  about supporting-article access or old information presented as new, through
  a readable support family and a publisher-app community. Success is a new
  cluster that strengthens or contradicts OPP-001/003; failure is only blocked,
  undated or duplicated material. Do not repeat general engines yielding generic
  homepages. Separately, a usability session could test the current labels and
  scope, but none was conducted or scheduled here.
