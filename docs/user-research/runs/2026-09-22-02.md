1. **OPP-001 remains first, now validate.** Problem confidence rises from low to
   medium: direct accounts establish a reason to investigate fidelity and
   verifiability across three families. Solution confidence stays low. First
   assess whether real supporting reporting can be captured and matched to the

2. **OPP-002 remains second.** Diverse scope/depth preferences recur. Existing
   Support already addresses limits; test comprehension before adding a screen.
   Source inspection also found stale help wording, a separate documentation
   concern. Research does not show that Newsworthy users misunderstand its scope.
3. **OPP-003 remains third.** This run adds a closer example of temporal confusion
   than the pilot's duplicate-alert report. Confidence is still low: one new
   support-thread account of this problem, with no Newsworthy usability test.
   Distinguish content accuracy, assessment time and development age; do not
   automatically turn the current `since` value into a sentence-age label.
4. **OPP-004: preserve.** Brief, intelligible checks and a working web route have
   support. Neither another feed nor compulsory onward reading follows.

## Limitations and retrospective

- Nine observations are a convenience sample. Identity independence cannot be
  established across sites. Kagi contributes five observations in four clusters;
  family diversity does not eliminate selection bias. No counts reflect market
  prevalence, clinical outcomes, willingness to pay or adoption of Newsworthy.
- Only E-07 has an exact recent publication date from result metadata. E-01/02
  have relative dates; Kagi evidence cannot be certified recent. E-06 is openly
  historical. Exact-date and non-English evidence remain gaps. Android coverage
  improved through Google Play but was historical; no current accessibility study.
- Versus the pilot experiment: targeted trust/source queries yielded useful
  cross-family problem detail; broad abandonment found one relevant cluster and
  much unrelated installation material. Positive searches were sparse; positive
  evidence emerged in inspected replies and store reviews. This is a qualitative
  yield comparison, not a controlled test of search effectiveness.
- Deduplication: all admitted clusters are new relative to run 01. E-04/05 share
  one cluster; repeated release complaints and direct-comment/parent views do
  not multiply support. No usernames or sensitive personal details retained.
- Skill change, requested by the owner: compare fetched main against the prior
  base before selecting a question and check intervening merges before closure.
  The initial checkout lacked the recently merged skill, demonstrating why this
  matters. Added reconciliation fields to the ledger format.
- Playbook change: follow request threads through replies/resolutions; E-08
  demonstrates that the requested feature can already exist. Use complaint
  language to locate source-access failures such as E-03, not just generic trust.
- Next search experiment: seek dated 2025–26 non-Kagi support/store accounts of
  source verification and temporal wording, including one non-English source
