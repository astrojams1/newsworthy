# v13: user-selected 140-character sentence budget

The user reported a clipped medium-widget sentence on 20 September 2026. The
public `/api/current` response matched the screenshot and contained 148 characters:

> Saudi Arabia said its air defenses shot down a Houthi ballistic missile aimed at Riyadh, the first attack on the capital since the Iran war widened.

The initial PR proposed 100 characters. The user rejected that cut as too
aggressive, discussed a 95% fit target, then explicitly chose **140 characters**
and requested merging the PR. The 95% fit study was stopped. The final v13 changes
only `150 characters` to `140 characters` relative to v12. Everything else in the
prompt, including rating instructions and writing guidance, is byte-identical.
Caller and OpenAPI descriptions agree; ingestion and saved readings are unchanged.

The 100-character draft was never deployed and no stored readings reference it.
It is revised within the unpublished v13 proposal, not added as a retired
production version. All published v1–v12 hashes remain unchanged.

## Initial experiment: 100 characters, not the selected limit

[Exact inputs, raw outputs and counts](v13.json) preserve the earlier experiment.
The JSON identifies `tested_100_character_proposal_prompt_text` separately from
`selected_prompt_text`. Its original `v13` experiment arm means the initial
100-character proposal; it is not evidence about the selected 140-character limit.

Seven source cards (six reconstructed v12 cases plus the Riyadh example) were
used for three fresh GPT-6 Astra high-effort CLI invocations per arm. An additional
100-character run, started during certificate failures, is retained separately.
Outputs were not repaired, truncated or discarded. Some invocations browsed and
added links despite the source-notes-only wrapper, so this was not a clean
controlled offline comparison or production-caller validation.

| Instructions tested | Within 100 characters | Range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| v12: 150 characters | 12/21 | 74–124 |
| Initial proposal: 100 characters | 21/21 | 74–98 |

The seven extra outputs were 73–96 characters. No output contained semicolons or
em dashes. Manual review found no clear factual or grammatical failure against
the supplied notes. Attribution, uncertainty and future tense survived; the
100-character proposal lost secondary details such as China and India in the
sanctions case. These are writing fixtures, not verified news or calibration data.

## Verification and release boundary

140 is the user's chosen budget, not an empirically selected 95% fit threshold.
No new generation-quality comparison was run at 140, and no native fit percentage
was measured. The exploratory native measurement setup was stopped when the user
changed the scope. Font sizes and widget layout are unchanged. Character count
cannot guarantee fit across glyph widths, two-digit scores, widget dimensions or
accessibility settings. Native widget fit remains unverified.

Regression checks enforce the selected prompt bytes, preserve v1–v12 hashes,
synchronize caller guidance and retain the reported clipping case. The earlier
100-character experiment remains separately identified and mechanically checked.
Deployment and adoption by a naturally arriving verified v13 reading are separate
release checks; existing saved sentences are not rewritten.
