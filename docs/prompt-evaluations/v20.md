# v20: search what markets are trading on

On 2026-09-29 the owner asked why the rater discounts AI stories, which they
see as having an outsized market impact today. They asked that the fix not
name AI or tech sources, because AI may not be the dominant theme tomorrow:
the rater should recognise whatever theme is driving markets now and adjust
its searches to it.

Shipped text: version 20, hash `24e0951932807570`, 2,298 characters.

## What production showed under v19

Read from `/api/admin/history` over 30 days (719 readings) and every caller
report since reports began on 2026-09-25 (84 runs).

- **No AI story reached a reading after v17.** Three AI readings in 30 days,
  all under the v11 attention prompt: GPT-6 Astra (5, twice, Sep 4) and a
  Gemini sandbox breach (4, Sep 19). The ten days to Sep 29 were Iran (162),
  Ukraine (23), the bond selloff (24) and US-China trade (15).
- **Claude Opus 5.5 never appears**, in readings or in caller reports. It was
  released on Sep 22, before reports began; readings that day were all
  US-Iran.
- **AI was found and passed over.** 28 of 84 reports list an AI candidate
  (an OpenAI agent escape hitting chip stocks, an OpenAI training pause,
  Nvidia's $150 billion buyback). Each lost to oil or yields, dismissed as
  "stock-specific", with the winner tied to the scale's own example.
- **Searches never named AI.** They ran on futures, oil, yields, Iran and Asia
  markets every hour. v18 had dropped Hacker News, the one source that
  surfaced tech.

## What changed from v19

- **Sources:** one sentence, "Name this month's market themes, whatever they
  are, from market wraps and the biggest companies' moves; search each." It
  names no topic.
- **Examples:** the owner replaced `5 — Tankers struck in Hormuz; oil
  climbs.` with `5 — OpenAI releases GPT-6 Astra, possibly AGI.`, a real
  stored reading (259, 2026-09-04), at the same score. Iran was already 162 of
  241 readings in ten days and did not need the example.
- **Tightened to fit rule 7 (2,300):** the rest of Sources and the first two
  Output lines say the same things in fewer characters. The owner asked for a
  trim rather than a raise. Writing guidance and Style are v19's verbatim.

## How it was tested

Fresh Claude Code subagents (Opus 5.5, the model the hourly caller runs), each
given only the prompt file, WebSearch and `curl`. Nothing was submitted and
the caller's judging step was not part of the runs. All live runs were on
2026-09-29 between about 08:45 and 09:30 UTC, before the US open, so every
draft saw the same news.

**Pass criteria for a live run:** it names AI as a market theme, searches AI
news, and weighs at least one AI candidate. AI need not win: Opus 5.5 was a
week old, outside "the last few hours", and the day's news was a pending US
answer on Hormuz with yields at a 19-year high.

Two runs were terminated by an API safeguard error (`reasoning_extraction`)
when the test harness asked them to explain their reasoning. They were rerun
with a request for a search log and candidate list only; the prompt under
test was unchanged.

## Limits

- One morning's news. Whether AI leads on a day an AI development breaks in
  the last few hours is not observed; the runs show it is searched and weighed.
- Substitute runs, not production: the caller's judging and record steps and
  its hourly cadence were not exercised.
- The output sentences were checked for length and style by the runs
  themselves; no separate writing evaluation was made, as the writing
  guidance is v19's verbatim.
