# v26: the preview leads only on the last day

Shipped text: version 26, hash `1f0ae05c4d45e0ea`, 2,299 characters (limit
2,300). Requested by the owner on 2026-11-16, to ship only once a backtest
showed it produced good changes.

## The problem

v23 (2026-10-14) added "Before scheduled news, lead with what it will settle"
with no window. Until November every preview it produced sat within about a day
of its event: runs of 2 to 15 readings for inflation data, the Fed meeting and
the midterms. Nvidia's results on Nov 17, after its CEO resigned on Nov 12,
were the first preview to stand for days. "Nvidia reports results Nov 17, its
first since its CEO quit" led 42 readings from Nov 13 19:00 UTC to Nov 16,
four days before the release, and what happened meanwhile (selloffs in Asia
and New York, draft chip limits, President Trump backing the CEO's return) was
squeezed into "as" clauses after it. Together with the caller's newer-in-story
check (#268) and judge v7, that put the same headline on the timeline again and
again ([judge v8 record](judge-v8.md)).

## What changed from v25

Output only. Everything before Output is v25's.

> Before scheduled news, lead with what it will settle.

became

> Within a day of scheduled news, lead with what it will settle; earlier, with what happened.

"Earlier, with what happened" is the replacement v23's lesson asks for: a
preview lead removed days out needs something to lead with instead. The clause
is 38 characters longer, and v25 was at 2,288 of 2,300, so four phrases were
tightened without changing what they ask:

- "Write calmly for a reader with no background:" became "Write calmly for a lay reader:".
- "At most one figure, in plain terms;" became "At most one figure, plainly;".
- "(no year if this one, no weekdays or relative days)" became "(no current year, no weekdays or relative days)".
- "rating justification, advice." became "score justification, advice."

Rule 11 in `PROMPT-RULES.md` now reads "lead with what it will settle, within
a day of it; earlier, with what happened."

## How it was tested

Eighteen fact notes in [v26-fixtures.md](v26-fixtures.md), each giving the hour
the sentence is written and the score already chosen:

- **F1–F8, far:** the scheduled event is more than a day away. F1–F7 are real
  hours from production (the Nov 10 inflation report from Nov 8, and Nvidia's
  results from Nov 14 to 16), written from the caller's run reports. F8 is
  v23's bond-auction note, 27 hours before the first auction.
- **N1–N5, near:** within a day. N1–N3 from production (the Nov 10 inflation
  report 3.5 hours out, the Nov 13 wholesale-price report 13.5 hours out, the
  midterms on voting day), N4 and N5 from v23.
- **C1–C5, controls:** news that happened, and v23's hurricane forecast, with
  nothing scheduled.

Each version's Output section and the notes went to three fresh Claude agents
(Agent tool, `opus` alias, the model family the hourly caller runs; harness
default settings; file read only, no web), one sentence per note. The agents
were not told what was being tested. Lengths, em dashes, semicolons and
relative days were then checked with code.

- N2 is weak in v25 A and B ("are due Nov 13, as traders bet…"), and leads

- N4 is weak in v26 B ("are the first since the Fed raised rates and come

## Findings

- The change does what it was for: across three independent runs, no sentence
  written more than a day before scheduled news led with it, against every one
  under v25, and each led instead with the hour's development.
- No loss within a day: 11 of 15 near sentences lead with the stake, against
  10 of 15.
- Controls read alike.
- One v26 run (B) gave no date for two near releases, "due at 8:30 a.m.
  Eastern" (N1, N4); every other sentence dates its release. Under v24 a date
  is what the style line asks; worth watching in live readings.
- Both versions misdated C5's "by Thursday" (Oct 8) as Oct 9 in two runs each:
  a weekday conversion error unrelated to the change.

## Limits

- Fixtures with a stated time, not the live caller. Whether the caller judges
  "within a day" correctly against its own research, and dates what it writes,
  is untested until verified live readings arrive (the v24 lesson).
- Three samples per cell; 18 notes; most far notes are one story.
- An event a day and a few hours away (F6 and F7, 28 to 29 hours) is the edge
  the clause draws; a release 20 hours out leads with the preview, one 30
  hours out does not.
- Scale and Examples are untouched, so scores are comparable with v25; the
  test did not score anything.
