# v14: include development age within 140 characters

The user selected a leading “31 hours ago:” and required the existing character
limit to include it. V14 leaves everything before Output byte-identical to v13.
It reserves 20 characters for an app-computed age prefix and asks for a body of
at most 120 characters. The full prompt is 1,978 characters. No rating scale,
calibration examples, ingestion rejection or stored-text truncation rule changes.

[Exact instructions, inputs and every output](v14.json) retain two experiments.
Both compare v13 and v14 on the same seven reconstructed source cards, with two
fresh generations per arm. The local Codex configuration selected GPT-6 Astra
with high reasoning effort; this is a substitute for the production Anthropic
workflow. Browsing was disabled. The event records show agent-message outputs,
not source research or tool use. These fixtures are not independently verified
news and do not establish score calibration or production adoption.

The initial wrapper requested a fixed score of 5 alongside each sentence. That
produced refusals to score political material, sometimes replacing the entire
array with a refusal. Those outputs are retained, not counted as successful
news sentences. The follow-up wrapper isolates sentence writing, explicitly
requests no assessment or score, and preserves the exact writing guidance under
test. This changes the evaluation wrapper, not the published prompt proposal.
It does not prove the full production prompt works in that substitute harness.

All v14 results remain within 140 with the full 20-character reserve. No outputs
were repaired or truncated. Review against the source cards found complete
sentences, preserved attribution in the Saudi and Polish reports, and retained
future/conditional meaning for planned pipeline repairs and tariff authorization.
No em dashes or semicolons appear. Shortening omitted some secondary details,
such as a further expected rate increase and the Riyadh attack’s broader context.
The selected development stayed the same across arms. The sample is too small
to establish a compliance rate, and it does not show a writing-quality improvement
relative to v13: both arms were already below 120 on these cards.

Mechanical and integration tests separately verify the 140-character combined
display budget, preparation receipt lifecycle, first-coverage root lookup and
prefix aging. Native glyph fit and installed-client behavior require separate
release verification; character count alone does not establish widget fit.
