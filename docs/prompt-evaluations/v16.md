# v16: 135-character body now that the only prefix is "New: "

The app no longer prints an age such as "31 hours ago: " before a sentence. A
sentence that opened its own development is labelled **New:**, in bold, for two
hours after its reading was saved; every other sentence has no prefix. v14 and
v15 reserved 20 of the 140 display characters for the age. "New: " needs 5, so
the user asked to give the other 15 back to the sentence.

V16 changes only the budget in Output: the body goes from 120 to 135
characters and the reserve from 20 characters for an age prefix to 5 for the
label. The writing guidance is v15's word for word, and everything before Output
is byte-identical to v15, so the scale, examples and calibration are unchanged.
The full prompt is 1,984 characters.

[Exact instructions, inputs and every output](v16.json) compare v15 and v16 on
v15's eight source cards. Each arm ran twice, each run a fresh Claude Opus
subagent with no web search, given its arm's Output section and the cards. That
differs from v15's runs, which were given the whole prompt; nothing before
Output differs between the arms. This substitutes for the production rater and
its research workflow. Scoring was not requested, so nothing here bears on
calibration.

Review against the cards: v16 used most of the extra room to keep what v15 had
cut. Both v16 runs said Greenland's troop and base numbers were not specified,
where neither v15 run did. Both kept the Fed's signal of another increase (v15:
neither). One kept that pipeline repairs are not finished. Neither arm tied the
tanker attack to fuel prices, which the notes do not support. Costs: sentences
run about 15 characters longer, so a new development's sentence can now fill the
whole 140-character display. One v16 output said supply worries "faded" where
the notes say "eased", and one called tariffs a tax on goods, which is plain but
looser. No outputs were repaired or truncated.

Two runs per arm is too small for a compliance rate. Whether live sentences use
the extra room depends on callers fetching v16 and returning a verified digest;
that adoption is not yet established.
