What is Newsworthy?

A global status indicator: a number out of 10 and one sentence explaining why. Higher scores mean more consequential news. The score fades as developments age.

The news can pull us toward constant anxiety or make us switch off entirely. Newsworthy offers a calmer middle ground: check the world, then get on with your life.

The rating looks old or won’t load

Check your connection, reopen the app, or refresh the website. If “Try again” appears, tap it. “Saved reading” means the last available rating is being shown; its original update time stays visible. If no reading has been saved, you’ll see a dash until the service can be reached.

Ratings are updated periodically, not continuously. A score may remain unchanged between updates.

Widgets and the app show different readings

Widgets keep their own saved reading and refresh on a schedule controlled by your phone. Battery restrictions and connection issues can delay updates. Check the date and time shown on the widget, and open the app for a fresh check.

On a supported mobile version, add Newsworthy through your phone’s home-screen widget picker. Widgets are not available inside the website.

I disagree with a rating

Newsworthy uses AI to assess public news. Ratings are judgments, can be wrong, and are not a complete account of events or an emergency-alert service. You can send feedback to the support address above.

Accounts, payments and privacy
