What is Newsworthy?

Newsworthy rates the significance of world news using AI. Each reading includes a score from 1 to 10, a brief explanation and an update time. Higher scores mean more consequential news. The score fades as developments age.

A bold “New:” before the sentence means this is the first time Newsworthy has covered that development. It stays for two hours after the reading was saved, and widgets may drop it a little later. It is not a verified event date. Other sentences have no label.

The rating looks old or won’t load

Check your connection, reopen the app, or refresh the website. If “Try again” appears, tap it. If a refresh fails, the last available reading keeps its original update time. If no reading has been saved, you’ll see a dash until the service can be reached.

Ratings are updated periodically, not continuously. A score may remain unchanged between updates.

Widgets and the app show different readings

Widgets keep their own saved reading and refresh on a schedule controlled by your phone. Battery restrictions and connection issues can delay updates. Check the date and time shown on the widget, and open the app for a fresh check.

On a supported mobile version, add Newsworthy through your phone’s home-screen widget picker. Widgets are not available inside the website.

Appearance and notifications

Open Settings from the gear in the top corner. Appearance can follow your device (the default) or stay light or dark. Widgets follow your device’s setting. On iPhone, you can instead keep a widget light or dark: touch and hold it, choose Edit Widget, then Appearance. Show timeline, off unless you turn it on, adds the past week’s developments below the reading. The privacy policy and this page are linked from Settings.

Notification delivery is not yet available in TestFlight. In the mobile app, notifications are off unless you turn them on. When on, Newsworthy sends one notification when the displayed rating reaches the minimum score you choose, which is 8 unless you change it, once per development. Nothing is sent for routine updates, and nothing interrupts you while the app is open. If your device refuses the notification permission, allow Newsworthy in your device’s notification settings and try again.

I disagree with a rating

Newsworthy uses AI to assess public news. Ratings are judgments, can be wrong, and are not a complete account of events or an emergency-alert service. You can send feedback to the support address above.

Accounts, payments and privacy

There’s no account to manage, subscription to cancel or in-app purchase to restore. For information about technical logs, local saved readings, notification registrations and support messages, read the privacy policy.
